# analyzereview — sentiment analysis of a submitted review: POST a review, the app analyses it and stores sentiment='positive'
# Captured: 2026-11-06 06:44 UTC
# Stack: Django :8000, dealers :3001, reviews :3002 (all local)

How sentiment is analysed in this build: djangoapp/views.py assigns sentiment by a
keyword rule — review text containing 'great' or 'excellent' -> 'positive', otherwise
'neutral'. No Watson NLU service is wired into this local build.
