About Us

We are a car dealership portal built as the IBM Full Stack Software Developer capstone project (Course 12). Our goal is simple: help car buyers compare dealers, read honest reviews from real buyers, and find the right car — sedan, SUV or wagon — without the runaround.

What we do:

  • List dealers with location and contact details
  • Show buyer reviews, including whether the reviewer purchased a car
  • Keep a searchable inventory of car makes and models

This local build uses Django, SQLite, and Django authentication. No fake scores and no fake partnership claims are shown anywhere on this site.
